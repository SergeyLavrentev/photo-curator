## Decision uncertainty boundary

The engine now stores evidence coverage separately from decision probability and computes robust
cross-model disagreement across Apple Vision, NIMA, MobileCLIP and validated Codex ranking on a
common album-percentile scale. Conflicting models reduce the uncalibrated reliability shown to the
decision engine, and the native inspector exposes the disagreement percentage and model count.

The raw-confidence contract change increments the decision model to version 6 and invalidates the
previous Platt calibration version. Without album-separated labels, UI continues to say that
calibration is pending and does not present the raw value as a probability. This closes the local
disagreement implementation, not the human-labelled Brier/ECE acceptance gate.
