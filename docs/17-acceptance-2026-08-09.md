## Recurrence prevention

The repaired build adds four independent controls:

1. helper markers are found anywhere in `CommandLine.arguments`, including after LaunchServices
   arguments;
2. an exclusive worker lock rejects a second process before opening the catalog;
3. deletion requires `confirmed=true` and a verified SQLite online backup;
4. destructive requests and completions are written to `destructive-actions.jsonl`.

An installed-binary test held one isolated demo worker open and confirmed that the second worker
failed before catalog access. The first worker then shut down cleanly over the versioned JSONL
protocol.
